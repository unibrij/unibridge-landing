About UniBridge

UniBridge helps teams manage payout routes for stablecoin settlement.

UniBridge coordinates managed payout routes across approved partners, helping platforms review the path, track execution, and collect payout evidence without UniBridge taking custody of customer funds.

Funding, custody, compliance checks, and final execution remain with the appropriate approved participants.

UniBridge focuses on route review, execution readiness, partner handoff, and payout visibility before broader scale.

Validation Signals

UniBridge is developed through partner review, controlled testing, security validation, and corridor-level execution evidence.

Partner-Based Infrastructure

UniBridge coordinates payout flows across approved funding, ramp, wallet, and execution participants without replacing their regulated roles.
