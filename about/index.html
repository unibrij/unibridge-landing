About UniBridge

Infrastructure for controlled payout execution.

UniBridge is building a non-custodial execution coordination network for regulated payout providers, ramps, and execution partners.

Company

UniBridge operates through dedicated legal structures in the United Kingdom and the United States, supporting provider onboarding, corridor testing, partner documentation, and execution review.

Operating status

UniBridge is currently operating under partner review, with provider integrations, live corridor execution evidence, private partner documentation, and a controlled test surface.

  • Live corridor testing
  • Provider integrations and partner review
  • Private API and partner documentation
  • Execution evidence collection

Operating boundary

UniBridge does not custody customer funds or operate as a consumer-facing remittance service. Custody, KYC, fiat payout, payment processing, and regulated customer obligations remain with the participating providers.
