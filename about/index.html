About UniBridge

UniBridge coordinates payout execution across approved providers.

UniBridge helps regulated payout providers, ramps, wallets, and execution partners commit the payout path before funds move.

The protocol supports partner onboarding, corridor testing, private documentation, and execution review without presenting itself as a public consumer remittance service.

Some flows may use transaction-specific funding addresses for observation and reconciliation, while execution remains governed by the approved provider path, user authorization, and corridor-specific operating rules.
