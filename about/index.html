About UniBridge

Non-custodial execution coordination for regulated payout infrastructure.

UniBridge is building a non-custodial execution coordination network for regulated payout providers, ramps, and execution partners.

The company coordinates payout execution paths across approved providers, supporting partner onboarding, corridor testing, private documentation, and execution review.

UniBridge operates through dedicated legal structures in the United Kingdom and the United States, with provider-side custody, KYC, fiat payout, and regulated obligations remaining with participating providers.
