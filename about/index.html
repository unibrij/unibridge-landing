About UniBridge

Founder-led infrastructure for controlled payout execution.

UniBridge is building a non-custodial execution coordination network for regulated payout providers, ramps, and execution partners.

The company operates through dedicated legal structures in the United Kingdom and the United States, supporting provider onboarding, controlled corridor testing, partner documentation, and execution review.

Company

UniBridge is structured for partner-led infrastructure review, not public consumer remittance launch. The operating model is built around provider-side execution, documented boundaries, and controlled evidence collection.

Operating status

UniBridge is currently operating under controlled partner review, with provider integrations, live corridor execution evidence, private partner documentation, and a controlled test surface.

  • Controlled live corridor testing
  • Provider integrations and partner review
  • Private API and partner documentation
  • Execution evidence collection

Operating boundary

UniBridge does not custody customer funds or operate as a consumer-facing remittance service. Custody, KYC, fiat payout, payment processing, and regulated customer obligations remain with the participating providers.
