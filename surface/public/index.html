How this controlled test works

  • Select the source country, destination country, and amount.
  • UniBridge prepares the execution path before payment begins.
  • After the quote, provide the required destination payout details, such as a local payout key or account information.

Before you pay

  • Payment is handled by the provider shown during checkout.
  • UniBridge does not process the payment or receive customer funds.
  • UniBridge does not collect card details or personal information in this controlled test surface.
  • Use only a small amount. If your bank or provider rejects it, stop.
