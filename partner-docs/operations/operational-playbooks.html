Operational Playbooks

Operational guidance for controlled UniBridge integration review.

Funding confirmed, execution not completed

Meaning:

  • Funding was observed or confirmed.
  • Settlement confirmation dispatch moves the funded settlement into the next operational step.
  • Execution has not reached final completion.
  • Status may be funding_confirmed, submitted, executing, or processing.

Partner action:

  • Do not treat the payout as completed.
  • Query settlement status.
  • Confirm settlement dispatch according to the approved flow.
  • Wait for provider status reconciliation.
  • Escalate if the status remains unchanged beyond the agreed review window.

Processing with provider reconciliation

Meaning:

  • Execution provider processing or reconciliation is in progress.
  • The settlement has not reached a final status.

Partner action:

  • Continue status polling.
  • Do not submit duplicate execution.
  • Treat as non-final until completed or failed.

Manual resume required

Meaning:

  • UniBridge requires operator review before continuing or closing the settlement.

Possible reasons:

  • Funding mismatch
  • Provider response unclear
  • Execution readiness failure
  • Retry safety check required
  • Provider terminal state requires reconciliation

Partner action:

  • Do not retry automatically.
  • Contact UniBridge with the settlement ID.
  • Wait for manual outcome.

Execution retryable

Meaning:

  • Execution failed before a dangerous provider-side finality point.
  • Retry may be possible under controlled conditions.

Partner action:

  • Do not retry unless instructed.
  • Confirm retry policy with UniBridge.

Failed

Meaning:

  • Settlement or execution reached a failure state.

Partner action:

  • Record settlement ID and status.
  • Request final evidence or remediation path if needed.
  • Do not treat funding confirmation as payout completion.

Completed

Meaning:

  • Execution submitted.
  • Execution provider reference recorded where applicable.
  • Provider status reconciliation confirmed success.

Partner action:

  • Treat as final success.
  • Store settlement evidence.
  • Reconcile internal partner records.
Intermediate statuses should not be treated as final settlement completion.
