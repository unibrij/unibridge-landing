Controlled Pilot Scope

The UniBridge pilot is limited to small-value, controlled payout testing.

The pilot is designed to validate whether a payout execution path can be prepared, funded, executed, and evidenced under controlled conditions before broader corridor activation.

Pilot characteristics

  • Small-value testing only
  • Limited partner access
  • Controlled test surface
  • Selected corridors only
  • Manual review where needed
  • No public remittance availability

Out of scope

  • Public retail launch
  • Production remittance service
  • Unrestricted customer access
  • Custody of funds by UniBridge
  • Card payment processing by UniBridge
  • Balance holding or prefunding by UniBridge
Successful controlled testing does not automatically create production approval. Production use requires separate written approval from UniBridge.
