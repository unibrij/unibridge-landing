Partner Access

UniBridge partner access is granted for controlled review, testing, and pilot evaluation.

Purpose

The purpose of partner access is to allow selected partners to understand how UniBridge prepares, tracks, and evidences payout execution across supported corridors.

What partners can review

  • Controlled test surface
  • Quote and payout preparation flow
  • Funding confirmation behavior
  • Execution status tracking
  • Evidence generated during a test
  • Operating boundaries and non-custodial model

Access limitations

Partner access does not create production approval, public availability, or permission to rely on UniBridge for live customer flows outside the agreed test scope.
