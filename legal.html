Partner Access Terms

The terms that apply when you use UniBridge partner tools, documentation, environments, and integrations.

Access may vary by partner, corridor, and environment. Production use requires the applicable onboarding, configuration, and approval for the relevant integration.

Last updated: September 2026

These Partner Access Terms govern access to and use of UniBridge partner tools, documentation, technical environments, integrations, and related materials made available by UniBridge Technologies Ltd. (“UniBridge”, “we”, “our”).

UniBridge provides a non-custodial cross-border payment coordination layer that connects funding, settlement, and local payout across supported providers and payment rails.

1. Your access

Partner Access may include documentation, development or review environments, hosted or embedded experiences, APIs, credentials, technical tools, and other integration materials depending on the services available to you.

  • Access may differ by integration type, country, corridor, payment rail, provider, and environment.
  • Access to one feature, route, or environment does not automatically provide access to others.
  • You must use Partner Access only for authorized business, technical, integration, and operational purposes.
  • UniBridge may limit, suspend, or revoke access where reasonably necessary for security, compliance, provider requirements, misuse, or operational risk.

2. UniBridge’s role

UniBridge coordinates payment workflows across the providers responsible for funding, settlement, compliance, custody, and local payout.

Unless expressly agreed otherwise, UniBridge does not hold customer funds or maintain customer balances.

  • Funding and payout execution may be performed by third-party providers connected to the relevant route.
  • Custody, regulated payment activity, identity verification, or other regulated functions may be performed by the provider responsible for that part of the workflow.
  • Access to UniBridge does not by itself change the legal or regulatory role of any partner or provider involved in a payment.

3. Production use

Development, sandbox, testing, or review access does not by itself authorize production use.

Production availability may depend on the relevant corridor, provider coverage, technical configuration, onboarding, compliance requirements, account status, and other operational conditions.

  • Use only the environment and credentials made available for your current level of access.
  • Do not use sandbox, testing, or review credentials for live customer transactions.
  • Production routes remain subject to provider availability, limits, supported payment methods, and route-specific requirements.
  • UniBridge may change or discontinue a route where a provider, payment rail, regulatory requirement, or operational condition changes.

4. Compliance responsibilities

Compliance responsibilities depend on the role performed by each partner and provider, the applicable payment flow, and any agreement governing that relationship.

UniBridge may apply route, jurisdiction, security, transaction, provider, or other operational controls before or during a payment workflow.

  • Partners are responsible for complying with laws, regulations, licensing requirements, and contractual obligations applicable to their own activities.
  • KYC, KYB, AML/CFT, sanctions screening, transaction monitoring, and related checks may be performed by UniBridge-integrated providers according to the role they perform in the relevant workflow.
  • UniBridge may restrict or discontinue access to a route or integration where required by law, provider policy, sanctions restrictions, security concerns, or material operational risk.
  • Nothing in UniBridge documentation or communications constitutes legal, tax, regulatory, or compliance advice.

5. Data and security

UniBridge may process and log information reasonably needed to authenticate access, operate integrations, coordinate supported workflows, troubleshoot issues, prevent abuse, and maintain service security and reliability.

  • Use production customer or payment data only through integrations and environments approved for production use.
  • Do not submit live customer data to sandbox, testing, or review environments unless that environment expressly supports such use.
  • You must take reasonable steps to protect credentials, access tokens, API keys, and other authentication material.
  • You must not share credentials with unauthorized persons or attempt to bypass UniBridge access or security controls.
  • By providing information through an integration, you confirm that you are authorized to provide and process that information for the relevant purpose.

Personal information is handled in accordance with the UniBridge Privacy Policy.

6. Payment information

Users and partners are responsible for reviewing the recipient, beneficiary, account, wallet, payout destination, and other payment instructions they provide before a payment is submitted.

UniBridge may validate required fields, formatting, supported routes, provider requirements, and operational readiness. Unless expressly stated otherwise, those checks do not constitute independent verification of the identity, ownership, or correctness of user-provided recipient or account information.

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, Partner Access, documentation, development environments, and related technical services are provided on an “as is” and “as available” basis.

  • Partners remain responsible for testing their integration and determining whether it is suitable for their own products and use cases.

9. Third-party services

UniBridge coordinates with third-party services that may provide functions such as authentication, identity verification, funding, custody, banking connectivity, settlement, blockchain infrastructure, or local payout execution.

The availability, eligibility, pricing, limits, processing times, and terms of a payment route may therefore depend in part on the provider responsible for that step.

  • Third-party providers may apply their own terms, privacy policies, compliance requirements, and eligibility rules.
  • UniBridge does not control every decision, restriction, outage, or service change made by an independent provider.
