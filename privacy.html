What we collect, why we use it, and the choices you have.

UniBridge Technologies Ltd. (“UniBridge”, “we”, “our”) provides website services, partner access, technical materials, communications, and related payment workflows. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use our website or provide information through our forms, services, or communications.

1. What we collect

We may collect:

  • Name

  • Company, organization, project, or role information you choose to provide
  • Messages and other information submitted through our forms, emails, or communications
  • Partner access, corridor, integration, or technical information you voluntarily provide

2. How we use it

We may use this information to:

  • Respond to inquiries, requests, and support messages

We do not sell or rent personal information.

3. Why we process it

Where applicable, we process personal information on one or more lawful grounds, including your consent, our legitimate interests in operating and securing our services, responding to your requests, performing or preparing for a contractual relationship, and maintaining records for business, legal, security, or compliance purposes.

4. How long we keep it

Information may be stored in:

  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure services

We keep information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including responding to requests, operating services, maintaining business and operational records, complying with legal obligations, resolving disputes, and protecting our systems.

These providers may process information on our behalf where necessary to provide their services.

7. International transfers

Information may be processed or stored outside your country of residence depending on where our infrastructure and service providers operate.

Where required, we take reasonable steps to apply appropriate safeguards to international processing and transfers.
