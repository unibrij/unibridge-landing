Privacy Policy

How UniBridge Technologies Ltd. collects, uses, stores, and protects information submitted through our website, partner access forms, and controlled evaluation pathways.

UniBridge uses submitted information to respond to inquiries, review partner access requests, operate controlled evaluation pathways, and maintain the security and reliability of its website and partner review process.

Last Updated: 2026

UniBridge Technologies Ltd. (“UniBridge”, “we”, “our”) provides controlled partner access to private documentation, technical materials, communications, and related website services. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use this website or submit information through our contact, partner access, validation, or inquiry forms.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Company, organization, project, or role information you choose to submit
  • Message content submitted through our forms, emails, or communications
  • Partner access, corridor, integration, or technical review information you voluntarily provide
  • Technical metadata such as IP address, browser type, device information, access timestamps, and referring pages
  • System logs generated through hosting, security, analytics, reliability, and operational infrastructure

2. How We Use This Information

We may use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Responding to inquiries, requests, and partner access submissions
  • Reviewing partnership, corridor, integration, or controlled validation requests
  • Operating, securing, debugging, and improving the website and partner access process
  • Maintaining service reliability, preventing abuse, and protecting infrastructure integrity
  • Communicating with prospective or current partners regarding access, evaluation, onboarding, or technical review
  • Maintaining records related to access-control, review decisions, and operational readiness

We do not sell or rent personal information.

3. Legal Basis and Legitimate Interests

Where applicable, we process information based on one or more lawful grounds, including your consent, our legitimate interests in operating and securing our website and partner access process, the need to respond to your requests, and the need to maintain records for business, legal, security, and compliance purposes.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Information may be stored in:

  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure services
  • Internal operational, communications, and partner review systems
  • Automated logs used for security, reliability, analytics, and diagnostics

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including responding to inquiries, managing partner access, maintaining operational records, complying with legal obligations, resolving disputes, and protecting our systems.

5. Cookies and Tracking

We may use essential website technologies, security logging, and limited analytics tools to operate and monitor website performance. We do not use advertising trackers or third-party marketing cookies.

6. Third-Party Service Providers

We may use third-party infrastructure and service providers for hosting, website security, logging, analytics, communications, form processing, documentation delivery, and operational support. These providers may process limited information on our behalf as necessary to provide their services.

7. International Transfers

Information may be processed or stored in countries other than your country of residence, depending on the location of our service providers and infrastructure. Where required, we take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate safeguards apply to such processing.

8. Your Rights

You may request, subject to applicable law:

  • Access to personal information we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion of information where legally applicable
  • Restriction or objection to certain processing activities
  • Removal from non-essential communications

To make a request, contact: ceo@unibrij.io

9. Data Security

We use reasonable technical, organizational, and operational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration, or disclosure. No website, communication method, or technical system can be guaranteed to be fully secure.

10. Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our website, partner access process, technical operations, service providers, or legal obligations. The updated version will be posted on this page.

11. Governing Law

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of England and Wales, unless applicable data protection law requires otherwise.
