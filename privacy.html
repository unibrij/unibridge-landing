Privacy Policy

How information is handled when you use UniBridge.

We use information only where needed to operate UniBridge, support users and partners, coordinate payments, and keep our services secure.

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how UniBridge Technologies Ltd. (“UniBridge”, “we”, “our”) handles information when you use our website, payment services, forms, support channels, or other UniBridge services.

1. Information we handle

The information we handle depends on how you use UniBridge. It may include:

  • Your name and email address when you create or use an account, contact us, request support, or submit a form
  • Company, organization, project, or role information you choose to provide
  • Messages and information you send through our forms, email, support, or other communications
  • Partner, corridor, integration, or technical information you voluntarily provide
  • Transaction information associated with payments made through UniBridge
  • Recipient, beneficiary, payout destination, account, wallet, or payment instruction information required to coordinate a payment
  • Technical metadata such as IP address, browser type, device information, access timestamps, and referring pages
  • System logs used for security, reliability, diagnostics, and service operation

2. How we use it

We use information where reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide and coordinate UniBridge services
  • Create, process, track, and support payment workflows
  • Provide payment history and receipts
  • Support features that make repeat payments easier
  • Respond to inquiries and support requests
  • Review partner, corridor, integration, or technical requests
  • Operate, secure, debug, and improve our services
  • Prevent abuse and protect our infrastructure
  • Maintain records where reasonably necessary for operational, security, legal, or compliance purposes

We do not sell or rent personal information.

3. Transaction history and Send again

When you make a payment through UniBridge, we may retain transaction records and recipient or payout instruction information associated with your authenticated account.

This information may be used to provide your payment history, receipts, support, reconciliation, and features such as Send again.

When you use Send again, information from a previous completed payment may be used to prefill a new payment. The previous transaction is used only as the source for that prefill.

A new payment remains a separate transaction and requires a new quote and a new settlement.

4. KYC and identity verification

Identity verification may be performed through third-party verification providers.

Where identity information must pass through UniBridge to complete a verification or related provider workflow, that information is processed only as needed for that purpose and is not retained by UniBridge as underlying KYC data.

UniBridge does not retain identity documents, document images, document numbers, selfies, biometric verification data, or raw KYC provider payloads as part of its customer records.

We may retain limited verification metadata needed to operate the service, such as verification status, provider and session references, and relevant timestamps.

This allows UniBridge to determine whether an authenticated user has already completed an applicable verification without storing the underlying KYC material itself.

Where required, UniBridge may check the current status of a prior verification with the verification provider before relying on it.

5. Why we process information

Where applicable, we process personal information on one or more lawful grounds, including your consent, performance or preparation of a contractual relationship, our legitimate interests in operating and securing our services, responding to your requests, and complying with legal obligations.

6. How long we keep information

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it is used.

Depending on the type of information, this may include periods needed to provide account functionality, payment history, Send again, receipts, customer support, reconciliation, security, dispute resolution, legal compliance, and operational recordkeeping.

Different categories of information may therefore have different retention periods.

Information processed transiently for identity verification is not retained as underlying KYC data by UniBridge.

7. Cookies and tracking

We may use essential website technologies, security logging, and limited analytics tools to operate our website and understand service performance.

We do not use advertising trackers or third-party marketing cookies.

8. Service providers

UniBridge works with third-party providers for functions such as hosting, authentication, identity verification, website security, logging, communications, payment funding, custody, compliance, and payout execution.

Depending on the workflow, information may be sent directly to or processed by the provider responsible for a particular step.

Those providers may process and retain information under their own legal, regulatory, and privacy obligations.

9. International transfers

Information may be processed or stored outside your country of residence depending on where UniBridge infrastructure and relevant service providers operate.

Where required, we take reasonable steps to apply appropriate safeguards to international processing and transfers.

10. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to request:

  • Access to personal information we hold about you
  • Correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Deletion of information where legally applicable
  • Restriction of or objection to certain processing
  • Removal from non-essential communications

To make a privacy request, contact support@unibrij.io .

If information relating to you is held directly by a third-party identity, payment, or compliance provider, certain requests may need to be made directly to that provider.

11. Security

We use reasonable technical, organizational, and operational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, misuse, loss, alteration, or disclosure.

No website, communication method, or technical system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our services, operations, providers, or legal obligations.

The current version will be published on this page.

This Privacy Policy is governed by the laws of England and Wales, unless applicable data protection law requires otherwise.
